Our Story

Crafting memories through the art of elegant gifting.

From our first hand-tied bow in 2010 to a global gifting atelier today, Curio has always believed that a gift is more than an object — it is a message, a memory, and a moment that stays forever.

The Curio Philosophy

Founded on the belief that every gift should tell a story, Curio has been curating premium souvenirs and luxury items since 2010. We travel the world to find artisans who share our passion for quality and design — from porcelain painters in Florence to silk weavers in Varanasi and lacquer masters in Kyoto.

Each piece in our collection is hand-selected, hand-finished, and hand-wrapped by our in-house atelier. Nothing is mass-produced; everything is made to be remembered. Our engraving studio personalises over forty items a day, and every parcel leaves our boutique with a handwritten card sealed in champagne-gold wax.

What began as a single boutique now serves clients in more than forty countries — yet every order still passes through the same careful hands that tied our very first bow.

Our Core Values

Quality

Only the finest materials and craftsmanship. Every piece is inspected by hand — twice — before it earns a place in our boutique.

Elegance

Refined designs that stand the test of time. We favour timeless silhouettes over passing trends, so your gift feels as beautiful years from now as it does today.

Sustainability

Ethically sourced and eco-friendly packaging. Our ribbons are silk, our boxes are recycled, and every artisan partner is paid fairly for their craft.

These three values guide every decision we make — from the workshops we choose to the wrapping we use and the people we thank along the way.
