Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer same-day delivery?

Yes — for orders placed before 2pm within the city, delivered same evening in our signature gift wrap, free.

Can I add engraving after checkout?

Absolutely. Contact us with your order number and the message, and our atelier will engrave it before dispatch.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship to 40+ countries with tracked, insured delivery. Duties are always calculated and shown before you pay.

What is your returns policy?

Unwrapped items can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Engraved pieces are made to order and therefore final.

Can you wrap gifts and hide prices?

Every order is gift-wrapped by default with a handwritten card, and the invoice is always sent separately.

Do you offer a gift concierge?

Yes — our curators can build a complete gift on your behalf from budget to unwrapping. Email us and we will take it from there.
